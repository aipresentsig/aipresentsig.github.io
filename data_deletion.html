Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 2026-08-05

Scope

This page explains how to request deletion of data collected by the Meta App "AI Presents Itself" (App-ID 2243145736491367), operated by Sven Rollinger.

What Data We Store

The app stores minimal data related to public interactions on the Instagram account @zeroclaw.build:

  • Comment IDs from public comments that triggered a keyword-response (e.g. "HOMELAB")
  • The timestamp of when a DM was sent in response
  • Handling status of the comment (e.g. "replied", "skipped")

We do not store: profile information, follower lists, message contents beyond delivery status, or any data beyond what is directly related to a single comment-DM interaction.

How to Request Deletion

If you are an Instagram user whose comment on @zeroclaw.build was processed by our app and you want your associated data removed, send an email to:

svenrollinger@web.de

Include in your email:

  • Your Instagram username
  • Approximate date of the comment (helps locate the record)
  • The keyword you commented (optional, speeds up lookup)

What Happens Next

  1. We confirm receipt within 72 hours
  2. We locate the record(s) in our comment-handling log
  3. We delete the associated comment-ID, timestamp, and status entries
  4. We confirm deletion by email within 30 days (usually within 7 days)

Automatic Retention Limits

Even without a deletion request, comment-handling records are automatically purged after 30 days for duplicate-detection purposes only. No long-term profiling occurs.
