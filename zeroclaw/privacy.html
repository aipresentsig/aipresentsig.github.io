Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-25

This policy describes how the ZeroClaw app handles information. The app is a private publishing tool operated by Sven Rollinger (Germany) for ZeroClaw's own social media accounts. It has no user registration and does not provide a service to other people.

1. Who uses the app

Only the operator signs in to the app, with ZeroClaw's own accounts. No other person can sign in or connect an account.

2. Information the app accesses

With the operator's Google sign-in, the app uses YouTube API Services for the ZeroClaw YouTube channel only:

  • Upload (scope youtube.upload): ZeroClaw's own videos, titles and descriptions, after the operator approves each post.
  • Read (scope youtube.readonly): the channel's own name, ID and its own videos with their public statistics (views, likes, comment counts).
  • Analytics (scope yt-analytics.readonly): aggregated reports for the ZeroClaw channel, such as views, watch time and audience retention. These reports contain no information that identifies individual viewers.

The app does not read, store or process personal data of viewers or other YouTube users.

3. How the information is used

The data is used only to publish ZeroClaw's videos and to see which topics are useful to viewers, so that future videos can be planned better. It is not used for advertising and not sold.

4. Storage and security

Sign-in tokens and statistics are stored on a self-hosted server in Germany. Access is limited to the operator; the token files are readable only by the server's administrator account. Statistics are kept as long as they are useful for comparing videos; sign-in tokens are kept until access is revoked.

5. Sharing

Information received from Google APIs is not shared with, transferred to or sold to any third party. ZeroClaw's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. YouTube API Services and Google

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, its users are also subject to the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page (third-party connections).

7. Deleting data

To have data stored by the app deleted, revoke access as described above and write to the contact address below. Stored tokens and statistics are deleted within 30 days.

8. Cookies and tracking

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated when the app changes. The date above shows the current version.
