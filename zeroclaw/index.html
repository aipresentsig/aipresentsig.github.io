ZeroClaw

ZeroClaw is a build-in-public project about running AI agents on your own hardware: local language models with Ollama, a small Proxmox homelab, and the automations around it. We share short videos and notes about what works, what breaks and what it costs.

The ZeroClaw app

The ZeroClaw app is a private publishing tool used only by the ZeroClaw operator for ZeroClaw's own accounts. It does not offer sign-up or any service to other people.

  • YouTube: uploads ZeroClaw's own short videos to the ZeroClaw YouTube channel after the operator approves each post, reads the channel's own video statistics (views, likes, comments, watch time) to see which topics are useful to viewers, and drafts replies to comments on ZeroClaw's own videos that the operator approves before they are posted. It uses the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API through Google sign-in.
  • Other channels: the same approved videos and texts are published to ZeroClaw's own Instagram, X and Bluesky accounts through their official APIs.
