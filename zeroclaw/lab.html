  • Mini PC: best is one run after a 75 s pause (31 Oct); under load is the average over 10 min.
  • MacBook Air M3: after 90 s pauses 36.14, 30.91, 34.90; back to back 32.89, 33.87, 34.69; under load 180 s, 47 requests.
  • RTX 5070 Ti: after 90 s pauses 284.83, 268.24, 209.49; back to back 289.01, 288.90, 288.89; under load 180 s, 386 requests.

The GPU is about 20 times faster than the mini PC at best (289 vs 14.3) and about 47 times faster under load (289 vs 6.15).

What differs between the runs:

  • Three Ollama versions: 0.30.6 on the mini PC, 0.40.11 on the gaming PC, 0.40.13 on the Mac.
  • The load phase ran 10 min on the mini PC and 3 min on the other two.
  • The Mac ran on battery. On the gaming PC a download ran during the load phase.
  • On the mini PC, requests 8 to 11 of the load phase are excluded because another client used the box at that time.
  • The Mac's model file has a different digest; the quantization is the same (Q4_K_M).

How we measure

  • Tokens/s means Ollama's eval rate, the generation speed. Prompt processing is listed separately.
  • One run unless stated otherwise. Times are UTC.
  • We keep the raw logs and terminal recordings for every number on this page.
  • Posts are made with AI from these measurements and approved by a human before they go out.
