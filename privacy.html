Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-05

This Privacy Policy explains how ZeroClaw ("we", "our", "the app") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with the Meta App "AI Presents Itself" (App-ID 2243145736491367), operated by Sven Rollinger.

1. Scope

ZeroClaw is a self-hosted content-automation tool operated by a single individual (Sven Rollinger) for the sole Instagram account @zeroclaw.build. The app does not offer services to third-party users, does not process third-party data, and does not sell or share any user data.

2. Data We Access

Through the Meta Graph API, the app accesses only:

  • Comments posted publicly on @zeroclaw.build content, to detect keyword-triggered CTAs (e.g. "HOMELAB")
  • Public post metadata (post ID, timestamp) for content moderation and CTA-response routing
  • The account owner's own Instagram Business account metadata (username, follower count, media count) for analytics

3. Data We Send

When a public comment on our own posts contains a registered keyword, the app sends a single Direct Message to that commenting user via Instagram Messaging, containing only the requested download link (e.g. a PDF guide). No promotional content, no marketing follow-up, no data collection from the recipient.

4. Data Retention

Comment metadata is stored on our self-hosted server (Proxmox LXC, Frankfurt, Germany) for up to 30 days for duplicate-detection purposes. No user profile data is retained beyond the comment ID and its handling status.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, license, or otherwise share user data with any third party. No analytics platforms, no advertising networks, no data brokers.

6. Security

All data is stored on a self-hosted server behind Tailscale VPN with SSH key authentication. API credentials are encrypted at rest. Backups are encrypted and retained for 14 days.

7. User Rights (GDPR / Art. 15-22)

If you are an Instagram user who commented on @zeroclaw.build content and want your comment-ID and DM-record removed, contact us at the email below. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.

8. Cookies and Tracking

This website uses no cookies, no analytics, and no tracking pixels.

9. Changes to this Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. The "Last updated" date above reflects the current version.
